What we see in Belgrade

  • Quotes that wait on the owner because he's the only one who knows the pricing.
  • POs and invoices typed from a PDF into QuickBooks, then again into a job list.

Each of those is a few hours a week. Across a year it's an employee you're paying for and not getting.

What we build

Small, specific software that takes one of those jobs off your desk, connected to what you already use. A quoting system that reads the part file. Paperwork that enters itself. A follow-up that goes out on its own. Here's what it looks like for a machine shop.
